Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This policy explains what Lumi ("we") collects when you use the Lumi mod menu, our Discord server, or this website — and what we do with it. Short version: we collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Discord account info. If you interact with our Discord server or bot, we see your Discord user ID, username, and messages in the server, as any Discord server does. Our bot stores moderation records (warnings) tied to your Discord user ID.
  • Mod usage. The mod may check for updates when it starts. That request includes your game version and mod version so we can serve the right update. We do not collect gameplay data, voice, or personal information from inside the game.
  • This website. This site does not use cookies, analytics, or trackers.

What we don't collect

  • No real names or emails. Purchases are handled through third-party payment processors — we never see or store your card details.
  • No voice chat, no gameplay recordings, no telemetry from your headset.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties. Ever.

How long we keep it

Moderation records stay while they're relevant to keeping the community safe. You can ask a moderator to review or remove your records where reasonable.

Third parties

Our community runs on Discord, so the Discord Privacy Policy and Discord Terms of Service also apply to everything that happens there.

Children

Lumi follows Discord's minimum age requirements. If you're not old enough to use Discord in your country, you can't use our server.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the update here and announce it in the Discord server.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Ping a staff member in the Lumi Discord server.
